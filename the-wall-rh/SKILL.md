# The Wall RH (Robinhood Chain Analytics & Explorer)

Guide for tracking ecosystem metrics, tokenized equities, launchpads, builder profiles, and address analytics on Robinhood Chain using thewallrh.fun.

## Core Features

- Stock Tokens Hub: Live tracking and pricing for US equities backed 1:1 on Robinhood Chain (NVDA, AAPL, AMZN, AMD, GOOGL, TSLA, MSFT, META, etc.)
- Launchpad Tracker: Monitor tokens launching across Robinhood Chain launchpads (Pons V2, Noxa.Fun, Pools.trade, Sushi Launchpad, bow.fun, LetsCash, ApeStore)
- Builder Profiles & Address Tracer: Search by contract address (CA), X handle (@), or wallet (0x) to analyze balances, transaction counts, deployed tokens, and claim histories
- Ecosystem Directory: Map out Robinhood Chain infrastructure, DEX aggregators (1inch, 0x), bridges (Across, Wormhole, LayerZero), and oracles (Pyth, Redstone)
- GameWorld: Track active web3 games and play statistics on Robinhood Chain

## Reference URLs & Endpoints

- Main Portal: https://thewallrh.fun
- Market & Stock Tokens: https://thewallrh.fun/market
- Block Explorer Integration: Blockscout Robinhood Chain instance

## Workflow

1. Equity Analysis: Check real-time 24/7 prices, 24h change, and volume metrics for Robinhood Chain tokenized stocks.
2. Builder Discovery: Trace deployer addresses and monitor new contract deployments across supported launchpads.
3. Ecosystem Navigation: Cross-reference protocol contracts and official bridge endpoints.
